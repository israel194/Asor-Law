1. General

This policy explains what information is collected on this website, for what purpose, to whom it is disclosed and what your rights are, in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981. The data controller is Asor & Co. – Attorneys at Law ("the Firm"), whose contact details appear above and at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Through the contact form: name, phone, email, subject and content of your inquiry.
  • Through the online legal service: the details required to prepare the document you ordered and to pay for it.
  • When contacting us by phone, email or WhatsApp: the details you choose to provide.

Technical details sent with a form inquiry: time of sending, page language, device and browser type, browser language, screen resolution, the page from which it was sent, the referring source (including campaign parameters) and time spent on the page.

This website does not use analytics tools, tracking pixels, or advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Providing information is voluntary

You are under no legal obligation to provide information; doing so depends on your will and consent. If you choose not to provide the required details, we will not be able to respond to you or provide the requested service.

4. Purposes of use

  • Responding to your inquiry and considering whether to provide you with legal services.
  • Providing legal services, including the online service, and ongoing communication with you.
  • Complying with the Firm's legal obligations.
  • Securing the website and preventing misuse.

5. Who receives the information

The Firm does not sell or rent personal information. Information is disclosed only to the following, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Web3Forms: the form-delivery provider that forwards contact-form inquiries to the Firm's mailbox.
  • Online service providers: the payment processor (SUMIT) and the server provider that processes orders (Cloudflare).
  • GitHub: the website hosting provider.
  • Google: only if you choose to display the embedded map.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to contact us that way.
  • Competent authorities, where required by law or court order.

Some of these providers may process information outside Israel.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website itself does not set cookies. Your browser (localStorage) stores only your accessibility preferences and your cookie choice.

The Google map embedded in the contact section may set Google cookies, so it loads only after you accept in the cookie notice or click "Show map". You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page, or delete stored data through your browser settings.

7. Data security

The Firm uses accepted security measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, absolute protection of information transmitted over the internet cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your rights

  • Right of access: you may inspect the personal information about you held by the Firm (section 13 of the Protection of Privacy Law).
  • Right to correction and deletion: if you find that the information is not correct, complete, clear or up to date, you may request that it be corrected or deleted (section 14 of the Law).

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

9. Data retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, and is then deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

The Firm may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be published on the website, and the date of the last update will appear at the top of the page.
